# Alistair’s Wildlands

Double-click `index.html` to play in a desktop browser. The game runs offline without installation or a server.

Create your hero by choosing a name, cloak, and two magic powers. The first playable space is a safe camp. You begin with a weak rusty sword and an Ice Staff, then practice against training dummies. Press Enter to open the travel map. Weapons found in later levels enter your inventory; gear, ammo, and potions carry forward.

The map has ten separate levels: Fernwood Trail, Amber Ridge, Mirewood Bog, Whisperwood Canopy, Frostglass Pass, Moonstone Ruins, Stormbreak Cliffs, Obsidian Quarry, Sunflare Causeway, and Ember Citadel. Each has distinct platforms, scenery, monster goals, and an exit gate. Sandjaw guards Amber Ridge as the first boss, the Moonstone Warden is the middle boss, and the Cinder Titan is the strongest final boss. Bosses have telegraphed special attacks and a faster second phase. The Warden appears after the level's monster goal is complete, so the fight begins without a pack of regular enemies. He has less health and damage, shorter volleys, and longer pauses between attacks; a rest stop before his arena gives you a chance to prepare. Platforms block hostile projectiles, and a boss health bar shows the damage you deal. Clear a level to unlock the next; press M to revisit the map. Chests and rest stops give supplies.

After clearing Ember Citadel, the final gate launches the Sky Run: a top-down airplane battle. Shoot down 18 drones, then defeat the sky ace to finish the whole game. Fly with WASD or the arrow keys, fire with J, Space, or a click, and pause with P. On a phone, the flight controls provide four direction buttons plus Attack and Pause.

Most levels have two gear finds; Moonstone Ruins, Obsidian Quarry, and Sunflare Causeway have three. Fernwood starts with a weak wooden club and pebble sling; Amber Ridge adds a Fire Staff and axe. Mirewood has a non-damaging wind staff for escapes and a sword upgrade. Whisperwood brings a Thorn Whip and pistol, then Frostglass adds an ice pick and another sword upgrade. Moonstone Ruins adds a shotgun and an Ice Staff upgrade, Stormbreak Cliffs has the machine gun, Obsidian Quarry adds the single-shot gun and Storm Staff, and Sunflare Causeway brings the hammer and Laser Staff. The Thorn Whip slows enemies with a broad melee lash. The Ice Staff freezes a hit enemy; frozen enemies cannot move or hurt you by touch. Fire Staff shots pierce across the level and set enemies burning. The Laser Staff has unlimited ammunition and no firing cooldown; its shots ricochet from level boundaries and platforms. Up to three shots can persist until you leave the level, and holding attack launches one per press. Later pickups upgrade existing weapons. Larger, tougher creatures resist knockback more strongly; melee weapons deliver stronger knockback than gunfire.

Spring pads launch you only when you land on them. Floor traps hurt grounded monsters and bosses as well as the player, so you can lure foes onto them. Traps cannot strike through an overhead platform, and crystal traps cycle between armed and safe. Rest stops activate only when you touch them on the ground.

Move with A/D or left/right arrows. Space, W, or up arrow jumps, including a double jump. Release early for a shorter hop. Hold J to attack ahead or click to attack toward the mouse. Q and F cast your two chosen powers: Ice Orb slows, Spark stuns, and Rocket Dash moves and strikes. Powers have cooldowns; you can swap them in the inventory. I opens the inventory and pauses play. Select a bar and slot there, then click a collected weapon to assign it; closing the inventory resumes play. B switches between two seven-slot bars, and number keys 1–7 select a slot on the active bar. E uses a health potion and cures ailments, P pauses, M opens the map, and R restarts after victory or defeat.

On a phone, turn to landscape. Use the left and right buttons to move, swipe up to jump or double jump, tap the game to attack once, and hold to keep attacking. Drag from Attack to aim. The two Magic buttons cast your chosen powers. Gear buttons cycle through collected weapons; Bar switches loadouts, and Pack opens the pausing inventory. Map leaves camp or opens the travel map; Pause stops the game. The phone layout fills the screen and accounts for display cutouts.

While playing a level, press F2 or click **Edit level** (the **Edit** button on a phone) to open the playtest editor. The game pauses while you place or drag platforms, traps, jump pads, chests, rest stops, supplies, and weapon pickups. Select an object to adjust its coordinates or width, or delete it. The editor also adjusts the monster goal, enemy health and damage, ground and flying speed, spawn interval, and the current level's boss health and damage. You can spawn a temporary monster for testing, pan the view, undo, or restore that level's original layout. Sandjaw now starts with 300 health and 11 contact damage, with slower attacks than before. Regular monsters spawn at most three at a time, farther apart and at longer intervals. Bats and wisps cruise at a steady height and warn before a dive aimed at your position at the start of the warning. A stationary player is hit by that dive, while moving out of its path still dodges it. Scorpions poison on contact, frostmites chill movement, and witch bats fire telegraphed lightning that briefly jams magic.

Edits take effect in the current game and save automatically in this browser's local storage, including when you reload. They are private to that browser and device; **Export edits** downloads a JSON file that can be imported on another device or shared for a future game update. Unlocks and current run progress are not saved by the editor.

The sprites in `assets/starter-pack` and `assets/monsters` load locally. `make-monsters.js` regenerates the monster and obstacle PNGs using `@napi-rs/canvas`; this build dependency is not needed to play. `node test-game.js` runs gameplay checks. `test-editor-browser.js` runs optional desktop and phone browser checks when supplied with Playwright and Chrome paths. `render-preview.js` produces offscreen visual snapshots when supplied with the canvas library. The original chess project in the parent folder is unchanged.
